Frequently Asked Questions

How does payment work?
When the customer returns from their trip, they pay through our secure system. You receive 92% instantly deposited to your bank. You keep 92% (we take only 5%, Stripe takes 3% for card processing).
What about after-hours returns?
Our lockbox system handles late arrivals. You set a code, leave the car key in your lockbox, and you text the code to the customer after payment. No overtime needed.

The flyer thinks of his most pressing car problem and enters it into Claude AI. That gives him a starting point for something to get fixed and you can upsell them from there. The smartest thing an owner can do is get everything fixed that there is time to fix.
